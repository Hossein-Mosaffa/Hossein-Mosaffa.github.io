About

I hold an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Control Systems) from K. N. Toosi University of Technology in Tehran, and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology. My thesis looked at how a networked control system, such as SCADA, can tell a physical fault from a cyber-attack.

I am now interested in bringing that model-based thinking, with its stability, robustness and fault detection, together with machine learning and reinforcement learning for robotic and cyber-physical systems. From 2022 to 2024 I also worked as an IT Specialist at the National Standards Organization of Iran (INSO).

  • Published a journal paper on separating faults from cyber-attacks in SCADA systems.
  • Presented two talks at the 8th International Conference on Control, Instrumentation and Automation (ICCIA), 2022.
  • Ranked 272nd of 83,262 participants in the Iranian national university entrance exam (Konkur) for Engineering, 2014.

Projects

Fault detection and isolation from cyber-attack in SCADA systems (M.Sc. thesis)

A method to tell physical faults apart from cyber-attacks in a networked control system. It combines fault-detection-filter residuals with network parameters such as delay and traffic, and was tested in OMNeT++ simulations.

Automated irrigation with soil-moisture sensors and solenoid valves on ESP32 nodes, connected over MQTT to a Raspberry Pi server with a web dashboard for live monitoring and manual or automatic valve control.
